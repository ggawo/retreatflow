Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do retreat participants need to sign up for accounts?

No. There is zero friction for attendees. Only the retreat coordinator creates an admin account. Participants receive digital invoices, registration receipts, and QR check-in badges directly via email.

How long does access remain open after the retreat?

With our Per-Event Pass, you maintain full operational access for 60 days after your retreat concludes to finish financial reconciliations and send follow-up surveys. After that, your data remains accessible in a read-only archive for up to 1 full year.

How does payment processing work?

RetreatFlow is built to integrate seamlessly with Stripe. During our development and launch phase, you can activate your workspace immediately with zero charges ($0.00).

Can we send customized emails with our group's name?

Yes! Every email sent through RetreatFlow dynamically displays your group or retreat title alongside the RetreatFlow verified header. You can also save bespoke email layouts as reusable templates.
